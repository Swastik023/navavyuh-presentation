## 🎯 Ground Rules (Dimaag me bas yeh 3 baatein rakho)

1. **Slide ko padhna paap hai:** Screen par visual proof hai, aapko bas uski summary 2-3 lines me bolni hai.
2. **Casual & Confident:** Jaise kisi dost ke bade bhai ya uncle se baat karte hain respect ke sath (*"Sir", "Aunty"*). No textbook English, no heavy Hindi.
3. **Point → Punchline → Next:** Cursor se point karo, 20 second me baat khatam karo, agle slide par chalo. Momentum banaye rakhna hai.
