## ⚡ The Executive Rule: Respect Their Time (Under 10 Minutes)
* **The 20-Second Per Slide Limit:** Speak 2–3 punchy sentences per slide, drop the **Founder Takeaway**, and advance immediately.
* **Never Read the Slide Text:** The slide shows the visual proof, your mouth gives the **business impact**.
* **Let the Founder Drive Depth:** If they want to discuss a slide, pause and answer. If they stay silent, keep moving swiftly.
* **Upfront Time Commitment:** Open by stating you will keep this under 10 minutes so they know you won't waste their time.
